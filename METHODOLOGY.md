# METHODOLOGY

## Participant Selection Process
The selection of participants for this study was conducted through a strategic approach to ensure diversity and relevance:

1. **Tech Collaborators**: We selected 36 tech collaborators who have demonstrated expertise in artificial intelligence and development. These collaborators were chosen based on their previous work, contributions to open-source projects, and academic qualifications.

2. **Meeting Participants**: An additional 26 individuals participated in various meetings related to the project's development. These participants were identified through networking within industry events and conferences.

3. **Experiential Reports**: We collected 9 experiential reports from participants to gain qualitative insights into user experiences and outcomes. Each report reflects various perspectives on using AI technologies in real-world scenarios.

4. **Telemetry Users**: The study included 21 users of Copilot with telemetry data. This group provided quantitative insights into the usage patterns and effectiveness of AI tools in development environments.

5. **Windsurf Users**: Lastly, we incorporated insights from 2 Windsurf users to further enhance our understanding of different applications of AI technologies in diverse contexts.

## Sample Composition
- **Total Tech Collaborators**: 36
- **Meeting Participants**: 26
- **Experiential Reports**: 9
- **Copilot Users with Telemetry**: 21
- **Windsurf Users**: 2

## Data Support for Mixed-Methods Analysis
The integration of qualitative and quantitative datasets supports a mixed-methods approach to analysis:
- The qualitative data from experiential reports offer in-depth perspectives on user experiences.
- Quantitative data, like telemetry from Copilot users, allows for statistical analysis of usage patterns, which are essential for validating qualitative insights.

Together, these datasets provide a comprehensive understanding of the impact of AI tools in collaborative environments, facilitating a richer analysis of the participant experiences and outcomes.
